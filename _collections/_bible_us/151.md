Esther 1 recounts the events that led to Queen Vashti's removal, setting the stage for God's purpose in the history of His people. Read the full passage {{ "here" | hyperlink: "https://www.bibliaonline.com.br/niv/et/1", true }}.

---

## Text Structure

### Est 1:1-9

The **great celebration**. Ahasuerus hosts lavish banquets to display his wealth and power, revealing the atmosphere of luxury and influence within the Persian court.

### Est 1:10-12

**Vashti's refusal**. The queen refuses the king's command to appear before his guests, triggering a crisis within the royal palace.

### Est 1:13-18

The **consultation of the advisers**. The wise men interpret Vashti's actions as a threat to male authority and recommend strict measures.

### Est 1:19-22

The **change in the palace**. The decree is issued, Vashti loses her position, and the story moves toward Esther's rise.

## Reflection

Esther 1 shows that seemingly ordinary events can prepare the way for greater purposes that we may not yet be able to see.

Human decisions carry real consequences and can alter the course of history, requiring wisdom and responsibility in the choices we make.

As highlighted in **Prophets and Kings**, chapter 49, God continues to work quietly behind the scenes, guiding events and preparing instruments to accomplish His purposes.
