Nehemiah 4 recounts the opposition faced during the rebuilding of Jerusalem's walls and the people's perseverance in the face of threats. Read the full passage {{ "here" | hyperlink: "https://www.bibliaonline.com.br/niv/ne/4", true }}.

---

## Text Structure

### Neh 4:1-3

The **ridicule from the adversaries**. Sanballat and Tobiah mock the rebuilding efforts, attempting to weaken the people's motivation through criticism and contempt.

### Neh 4:4-6

The **response in prayer**. Nehemiah brings the opposition before God, and the people continue working, demonstrating trust and perseverance in the midst of hostility.

### Neh 4:7-9

The **growing threat**. The enemies plan attacks to stop the work, but the people respond with vigilance and continual prayer.

### Neh 4:10-14

The **fight against discouragement**. Weariness and fear spread among the people, and Nehemiah strengthens them by reminding them of God's care and power.

### Neh 4:15-18

The **balance between work and protection**. The builders continue their work while remaining prepared to defend the city.

### Neh 4:19-23

The **unity in the mission**. The people remain mobilized and alert, demonstrating collective commitment until the task is completed.

## Reflection

Nehemiah 4 shows that every meaningful work faces opposition. Criticism, threats, and discouragement arise, but they do not have to stop what God has placed in our hands.

The people's attitude reveals that faith does not eliminate responsibility. They prayed, remained watchful, and continued working with perseverance.

As highlighted in **Prophets and Kings**, chapter 53, the rebuilding required courage, cooperation, and trust in God, teaching that great challenges are overcome when we remain steadfast in the mission.
